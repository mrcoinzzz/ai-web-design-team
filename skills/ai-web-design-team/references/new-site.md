# Building from scratch

Convert the brief into a small page/flow plan before coding. Identify the audience, offering, primary action, required pages, content sources, brand direction, and integration requirements. Infer reversible visual choices; ask about missing business facts that would change the site substantially. If the brief is only “build a website,” obtain its purpose and audience before inventing a business.

For each page, specify the visitor question it answers, the information sequence, and the next action. Define real destinations and required interaction states. Keep the scope proportional: a landing page request does not imply accounts, a CMS, payments, or a backend.

Use the existing stack if the workspace has one. For an empty project, choose the simplest suitable implementation given the user’s requirements and available environment. Explain significant dependency or architecture choices briefly. Do not force a universal framework.

Choose a concrete visual direction: typography roles, color roles, spacing, content width, image treatment, and interaction tone. Use available brand assets appropriately. Use clearly marked draft copy for unknown facts; do not make unsupported proof or pricing look real. Prefer omitting unsupported claims over filling layouts with fabricated credibility.

Build a representative page or section using actual content structure, review it through the three role lenses, and extend the direction across the requested pages. Include mobile behavior and required loading, empty, error, success, and focus states where relevant.

If a real integration is missing, implement the safe local work and identify the missing dependency. Do not make a form display a false submission success or present a simulated checkout as live. A requested prototype may simulate behavior when clearly labeled; distinguish that from a completed integration in the handoff.

There is no before screenshot. Deliver the brief-to-design rationale, working preview, implemented scope, and verification evidence instead.
